# Third-party resources

## Behringer Neutron artwork

The section tabs, brand graphics and tuner background in `assets/*.png` were derived from the supplied Neutron 1.0.3 application's SVG resources. They retain their original ownership and branding; this repository does not grant a new license for those resources or imply official endorsement.

## Cuprum fonts

`assets/Cuprum-Regular.ttf` and `assets/Cuprum-Bold.ttf` were reconstructed from the Cuprum WOFF files bundled with that application. Font attribution and embedded licensing notices are retained in `third-party/Cuprum-notices.txt`; the upstream license is included in `third-party/Cuprum-OFL.txt`.

## Rust dependencies

The exact dependency versions are recorded in Cargo.lock. Each dependency retains its own license; consult the crates' package metadata and license files.
